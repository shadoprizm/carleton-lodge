# Ready-to-send external image requests

## Huntley Township Historical Society

**Subject:** Carleton Lodge No. 465 — request for historical Carp photographs

Hello,

I'm writing on behalf of Carleton Lodge No. 465 in Carp. We are rebuilding the historical section of our Lodge website, carpmasons.ca, and are trying to document the Lodge's history using authentic archival photographs wherever possible.

The Carp Heritage Walk credits the Huntley Township Historical Society for many of the historical images used in its Carp material. We would like to ask whether HTHS holds high-resolution copies of any of the following:

- the Kidd Block, circa 1910, where Carleton Lodge originally met upstairs over the drug store;
- Kidd Street/Main Street streetscapes showing the Kidd Block;
- the Orange Hall and Town Hall area, circa 1900;
- the Carp Drug Store / circa-1908 postcard associated with the Kidd Block;
- early photographs of St. Andrew's Presbyterian Church, now the Carleton Lodge Masonic Temple;
- any photographs of, or immediately following, the July 20, 1920 Carp fire;
- George N. Kidd or other founding members of Carleton Lodge;
- any photographs, clippings or archival material specifically associated with Carleton Lodge No. 465.

We would be grateful for any additional Carleton Lodge-related material in the collection that we may not know to ask for.

For photographs that are available, could you please advise:

1. whether a high-resolution digital scan is available;
2. the known date, caption and provenance;
3. whether Carleton Lodge may reproduce the image on carpmasons.ca as part of our historical archive;
4. the preferred credit line;
5. whether there are any reproduction fees; and
6. whether we may crop or resize the image solely for responsive website presentation while preserving the historical content.

We would be pleased to credit HTHS prominently with each image and in the site's historical acknowledgements.

Thank you,

Jeramy Ratelle
Carleton Lodge No. 465
Carp, Ontario
carpmasons.ca

## Ottawa District One — District Photography request

**Subject:** Carleton Lodge No. 465 — request for District photo archive originals

Brethren,

Carleton Lodge No. 465 is expanding the historical archive on carpmasons.ca. We have found a number of Carleton Lodge photographs reproduced in Ottawa District One histories and galleries and would like to preserve the best available original files rather than extracting lower-resolution copies from the published PDFs.

Could this request please be forwarded to the District Photography team?

We are looking for any original/full-resolution photographs of Carleton Lodge No. 465 held in the District archive, particularly:

- DDGM official visits;
- installations and officer photographs;
- Lodge interior/exterior photographs;
- centennial or anniversary material;
- the 2016 West Carleton War Memorial/cornerstone activities;
- District and fraternal visits involving Carleton;
- any older photographs that pre-date the current online galleries.

We would like to reproduce selected images in the historical section of carpmasons.ca. Please also let us know the preferred photographer/District credit for each image and confirm that website reproduction by Carleton Lodge is acceptable.

Thank you,

Jeramy Ratelle
Carleton Lodge No. 465
