# Open Research Questions

These items should remain flagged in the site data until resolved.

## 1. Exact church deed / transfer date

Known:
- Church Union occurred in 1925.
- Ottawa District history says the former Presbyterian congregation agreed to deed the church and land to the Freemasons for $250 plus legal fees.
- Renovation followed.
- Carleton Lodge held its first meeting in the present Temple on 15 April 1927.

Need:
- deed
- land registry record
- Presbyterian trustee minute authorizing transfer
- Lodge minute accepting/completing transfer

Best lead:
Library and Archives Canada, Carp United Church fonds, microfilm M-816/M-817, including St. Andrew's trustee minutes to 1929.

## 2. Complete 23 charter members

The District history prints only a partial list in the main Carleton section. A separate Goodwood history identifies nine Goodwood members as charter members.

Need:
- 1904 Grand Lodge return
- institution record
- charter/warrant paperwork
- early Lodge register

## 3. July 20, 1920 fire — contemporary newspaper coverage

Need:

Goal:
- confirm precise date

## 4. St. Andrew's construction chronology

Carp Heritage Walk uses 1876.
The current carpmasons.ca text previously used 1872–1875.

Need:
- trustee minutes
- church dedication notice
- local newspaper
- denominational records

Until resolved, do not publish the old 1872–1875 sequence as fact.

## 5. Le Havre archive physical status

Ottawa District history's bibliography states that the Minute Book and Register of Members of La Loge Le Havre de Grâce No. 4 were held in the archives of Carleton Lodge.

Need:
- physically locate
- assess condition
- inventory documents
- conservation-safe digitization
- determine any Masonic privacy/restriction considerations before public release
